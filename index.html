Rambam — One Chapter a DaySupport

A free app for iPhone and iPad that brings the Rambam's Mishneh Torah one chapter a day, illustrated and read aloud. We made it as clear and engaging as we could, so that it is a pleasure to keep up every day.

Questions and feedback

Write to jonathan@bymagellan.co. Corrections to the translation or the pictures are especially welcome; please name the chapter and the page.

Good to know

  • One chapter a day. Each section of Sefer HaMadda is a map with one stop per chapter. A new chapter opens every day, and every chapter you have read stays open.
  • Shabbat and Yom Tov never count as missed days, and the daily reminder never comes on them.
  • iPhone and iPad. With iCloud turned on, your progress follows you between your own devices.
  • Narration. Every page can be read aloud in full or as a short summary; tap the ♪ button in a chapter to choose.
  • Privacy. No account, no ads, no tracking. See the privacy policy.
