Rambam — One Chapter a DayPrivacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026.

Rambam is made by Magellan Artificial Intelligence Systems GmbH, Lucerne, Switzerland.

What we collect

Nothing. The app collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing to us. Only a feedback mail you choose to send reaches us (see Feedback below).

What stays on your devices

Your reading progress, streak and settings are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, the app keeps them in step between your own devices through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, under your Apple account; we have no access to it. Daily reminders, if you turn them on, are scheduled on your device.

Links

The app's only web link leads to our website, bymagellan.co, and opens only when you tap it. If you share your rank card, the iOS share sheet sends it only where you choose.

Feedback

If you send us feedback from Settings, the app opens your mail app with a message to us. It goes from your own mail account, and only if you send it. Below your words it lists the app version, the iOS version, your device model, your reading settings and the last page you opened; you see these lines before sending and can delete them. We use your message only to answer you and to improve the app.
